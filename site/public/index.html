Self-checkout for shops without an IT department

Teach the till a product in thirty seconds.

A camera looks at what the customer put down. The till matches each item against the products it has been taught and checks the basket by weight. A new product is five photographs and a price. No retraining, no model, no data team.

How a sale runs

  1. 1

    Put it down

    The customer sets the items on the mat. One press of SCAN watches a few frames and looks each item up in the product gallery.

  2. 2

    Check the read

    Each product appears with its name and price. An item the till has never seen is flagged as unknown, not charged as the nearest thing, and can be taught on the spot.

  3. 3

    Pay

    The basket weight is checked against the cart, a PromptPay QR is shown for the total, and on confirmation the sale is written and stock comes down.

What it refuses to do

Most of the engineering went into the things a naive camera till gets wrong.

  • Charge for a guess. Below a calibrated similarity the till says unknown and offers to teach the product, instead of naming whatever it resembles.
  • Sell alcohol at the wrong hour. 11:00 to 24:00 only, and never without a member of staff confirming the buyer is 20 or over.
  • Show tobacco to a customer. Staff-only, never displayed, as Thai law requires.
  • Keep a picture of you. No frame is ever written to disk. Only a product's numbers persist. A test fails if anyone changes that.
  • Trust the camera alone. The basket on the pan must weigh what the cart says it should, within a tolerance set from measurement, not taste.

iPhone

Build it yourself

The whole till runs on an iPhone with its own camera: teach, scan, cart, PromptPay, inventory. It is not on the App Store, so you build it once with Xcode and a free Apple ID.

  1. Clone the repository on a Mac with Xcode 26.
  2. Open ios/AICashier, sign in with your Apple ID under Signing.
  3. Plug in the iPhone, turn on Developer Mode, press Run.

A free Apple ID installs for seven days at a time; a paid developer account lifts that. Details in ios/README.md.

The research behind it

The till is a retrieval system, not a classifier: propose, embed, match against a gallery, fuse with weight and size. The repository carries the experiment harness (E1 to E9), a paper draft with a verified bibliography, a dossier on Thai law and the self-checkout market, and a review of every architectural decision with its evidence.

  • The dossier law, market, how the commercial systems work, literature, venues
  • The architecture review nineteen decisions, kept or changed, with sources
  • The paper draft tables and figures are generated by the harness, never typed
  • Privacy what the cameras see, keep, and never keep
