How a sale runs

01

Put it down

The customer sets the items under the camera. One press of SCAN watches a few frames and looks each item up in the product gallery.

02

Check the read

Each product appears with its name and price, drawn on the camera frame. An item the till has never seen is flagged as unknown, not charged as the nearest thing, and can be taught on the spot.

03

Pay and it books itself

The basket weight is checked against the cart, a PromptPay QR is shown for the total, and on confirmation the sale is written, stock comes down, and this page moves.
