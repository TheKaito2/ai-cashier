## C. What each audience rewards (so the same work is framed three ways)

- **Science-fair judges (YSC/ISEF):** a question, a controlled experiment, error bars, and honesty about limitations. Lead with E2/E5/E6 and the flavour-variant failure.
- **ICT-award judges (TICTA/Conrad):** the market story in 03, the legal gates in 01, a demo that a judge can teach a new product in a minute, and a unit-economics table.
- **Reviewers (ECTI-CON/JCSSE/PRAW):** related work that cites GroZi 2007, NVIDIA's reference design and Vaze 2022; a public benchmark number; reproducible harness; a claim that is measurement, not novelty.
