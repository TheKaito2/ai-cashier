## Camera notice

This checkout uses a camera to **recognise products on the mat**.

- The camera looks at the mat, not at you.
- No video is recorded. There is no face recognition and no biometric data.
- The only records kept are what was bought, when, and the amount.
- Legal basis: the shop's legitimate interest under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562.
